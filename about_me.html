About
Me

Hi there! I’m Nahla Niavarani, a UX/UI and Product Designer based in Vancouver, with a love for both technology and creativity that has shaped my path.✨

With a degree in biomedical engineering, I started my career in the medical technology field. But my curiosity led me to explore multimedia and design through self-initiated projects and client work. Eventually, I returned to tech with a new lens, combining analytical thinking and creative problem-solving as a UX/UI designer.

In my role as a designer, I’ve collaborated with small and medium-sized companies to enhance user experiences across various sectors, including e-commerce, SaaS workflow management, and AI-powered platforms. I’ve also assisted startups in developing end-to-end products and services, focusing on creating accessible and scalable solutions through collaboration, research, and empathy.



I believe in iteration over perfection. Every project is a chance to learn, refine, and build something more meaningful. Let's connect if that resonates with you.
